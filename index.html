[ 01 / 04 ] · About

From theory to decisions

Third-year Industrial Engineering & Operations Research undergraduate at IIT Bombay, ranked second in the department, minoring in Statistics and CMInDS.

My work sits where optimization meets data. I like problems that start as a messy real-world question and end as a model you can trust — whether that is a mixed-integer program, a stochastic process or a neural network.

At CSALT, with Prof. Preethi Jyothi, I research fair evaluation of large language models across Indian languages. I have also built graph-based hallucination detectors, trained binarised neural networks with integer programming, and written telemetry firmware for the IITB Rocket Team.

Outside research I teach — as a teaching assistant for Probability, Calculus and Optimization — and represent IEOR's undergraduates as the department's Undergraduate Representative.
